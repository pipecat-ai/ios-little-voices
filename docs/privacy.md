# Little Voices privacy policy

Little Voices runs entirely on your device and collects no data.

- **Your voice.** When a conversation is on, the app uses your microphone and recognizes
  your words with Apple's on-device speech recognition; holding to talk, only what you say
  while you hold the microphone, and a moment either side. Audio is not recorded or stored,
  and it never leaves your device.
- **Conversations.** The angel's and the devil's replies are written by Apple Intelligence on
  your device and spoken by a voice model inside the app. The text of each conversation, what
  you said and what they said, is saved on your device so you can read it again; it is
  excluded from backups and never leaves the device. You can delete a conversation from the
  Conversations list, and delete them all or stop keeping them in Settings.
- **Your name.** If you give the app your name in Settings, typed or picked from Contacts, it
  is stored on your device so the characters can greet you. Picking a contact reads only that
  contact's first name. You can change or clear it in Settings at any time.
- **No network, no tracking.** The app makes no network requests of its own and contains no
  analytics, advertising or tracking. iOS may download Apple's speech recognition assets the
  first time you talk.

Questions: contact the developer through the App Store listing.
